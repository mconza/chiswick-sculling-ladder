What is it?

The Chiswick Sculling Ladder provides an opportunity for scullers with a wide range of abilities from the various clubs in the Chiswick area to train together on a regular basis.

We meet at Richmond Lock and scull back to Chiswick Bridge, starting slowest first. It is not a race and no times are recorded — it's a fun way to develop sculling skills.

208 scullers from over 20 different clubs have taken part making a total of 1492 finishers in 78 ladders so far.

How does it work?

  • Slowest first, fastest last — not a race. Safety first.
  • After your piece, report whether the sculler behind you caught up.
  • If not caught, your rank improves and you start later next time.
  • Comply with the Tideway Code.
  • No more than 2 boats abreast, especially near bridges.
  • Join the WhatsApp group for updates and cancellations.
