Rules Recap

  • Slowest first, fastest last — not a race. Safety first.
  • Comply with the Tideway Code.
  • No more than 2 boats abreast, especially near bridges and Oliver's Ait.
  • Capsize: following scullers stop and check the sculler is safe.
  • Don't stop under Chiswick Bridge. Use standard crossing points.
  • Join the WhatsApp group for updates and cancellations.
